\section*{BODY-MATTER}

\subsection*{\texttt{\_blank} --- Fixed}
\noindent
A viewport is examined here \emph{as an aperture}. The designation
\texttt{\_blank} and the quality \emph{Fixed} are the Architect's
initial pairing. The question carried by the workbook is:

\begin{quote}
What must remain stable for an aperture to retain its distinguishability?
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Fixed qualities --- bounded first articulation}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stable designation.}
  The aperture remains distinguishable as \texttt{\_blank}.
  Its designation is not silently replaced by an informal default.

  \item \textbf{Distinct receiving context.}
  The established HTML target keyword \texttt{\_blank} supplies a
  relevant precedent: it requests navigation in a new browsing context.
  Here that precedent is a point of reference for the aperture, not a
  claim that this TeX source implements browser navigation.

  \item \textbf{No fixed geometry implied.}
  Fixed does not, by this designation alone, prescribe width, height,
  aspect ratio, page dimensions, content, or positional allocation.

  \item \textbf{No standing conferred.}
  Making a receiving context available does not itself establish the
  orientation, validity, authority, or epistemic standing of what may
  appear within it.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
These are initial candidate qualities for examination, not a completed
formalization of Fixed. They do not install or alter a renderer.
