## Starting point

**We begin with what we have, not where we want to be.**

This is a rough orientation for an emerging library within the `portussophia-assets` repository. It describes a proposed way to receive and make existing representations inspectable. It is not an assertion that the full collection, its classifications, or its future organization has already been established.

The library's initial contents will be considered as they are received. A proposed category does not by itself determine the standing of an item placed within it.

## Five initial sections

The Architect has identified five initial library sections:

| Section | Provisional description — pending VERN review |
| --- | --- |
| **Sandbox** | Material received or held for exploration, with disposition open. |
| **Draft** | Material under development or refinement. |
| **Distributed** | Material that has entered some form of circulation or distribution. |
| **Published** | Material with an identifiable publication event or record. |
| **Canonical** | Material explicitly designated as authoritative *within a stated scope*. |

These are **initial interpretive descriptions**, not verified VERN definitions and not automatic standing assignments.

### Distinct, potentially overlapping relations

The five sections are **not solely mutually exclusive**. An item may be distributed and canonical, for example. More than one section may accurately describe the same item, but overlap must be supported by the item's actual provenance and standing.

```text
DISTRIBUTED
    ≠ NON-CANONICAL

PUBLISHED
    ≠ AUTOMATICALLY CANONICAL

CANONICAL
    ≠ EXCLUSIVELY PUBLISHED

REPOSITORY PRESENCE
    ≠ PUBLICATION STANDING
```

The sections must not be presumed to form a mandatory sequence from Sandbox to Canonical. Refinement, distribution, publication, and canonical designation may concern different relations. Whether these should be called *stages*, *states*, *sections*, or something else awaits examination of **VERN**.

## Receiving the initial assets

The first work is to identify the actual files and representations supplied, rather than arrange them according to an anticipated final architecture. For each received item, the library may describe, where the source permits:

- **What exists:** the supplied artifact or representation and its present form.
- **What is established:** attributable origin, version, distribution or publication record, and any expressly declared standing.
- **What remains open:** uncertain provenance, unverified status, possible relationships to other representations, or pending review.

These are observational prompts for the draft, **not a mandatory submission template**. Missing information stays marked as missing rather than supplied by inference.

Receiving or placing a file in the public assets repository can make it publicly accessible. That technical fact does not, by itself, establish a publication event, canonical standing, or permission to revise the work.

A later representation may clarify or refine an earlier one without silently replacing the earlier return or its provenance. Multiple representations of one work may carry different functions and standings.

## Architectural consideration — form and degree of consideration

A **Post-it note** may or may not receive the same level of consideration as a well-formatted book in a proper rendering.

The library does not establish an automatic hierarchy of attention from an artifact's length, polish, medium, or presentation. Nor does it require that differently presented artifacts receive equal consideration in every inquiry.

A brief note may carry an originating question, observation, or distinction that warrants close examination. A properly rendered book may carry sustained argument, context, and developed relations that warrant a different kind or duration of examination. Either may be consequential, inconsequential to a particular question, or not yet assessable.

```text
FORM OR FINISH
    ≠ PREDETERMINED DEGREE OF CONSIDERATION

BRIEF REPRESENTATION
    ≠ NECESSARILY LESSER CONSEQUENCE

EXTENDED CONSIDERATION
    ≠ CANONICAL STANDING
```

The appropriate level and kind of consideration remain local to the artifact, its provenance, the question under examination, and the circumstances of reception. Consideration is not the same as endorsement, adoption, or canonization.

This is an **architectural consideration for library reception**, not a scoring system, prescribed review effort, or obligation to treat unlike artifacts uniformly.
